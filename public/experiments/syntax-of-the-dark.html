About the landing page

Syntax of the Dark

This page is a piece of interactive fiction built on a real finding. In 2022 the computer scientist Andrew Adamatzky recorded electrical spiking in four species of fungi and reported that the spikes cluster into recurring patterns. Whether those patterns amount to anything like language is disputed.

Here a probe drifts through an imagined fungal network twelve kilometres down. You can look around but you cannot steer. Marked junctions carry readings. Something else lives down here and feeds on the network. None of it is aware of you.

Controls

Look
Move the pointer, or drag on a touch screen.
Read
Hold the reticle on a marked junction.
Esc
Skips the opening.

Prototype v1, for review

T
Opens tuning: drift speed, fog density, dither scale, grain and exposure.
C
Forces the convergence event instead of waiting for it.

Built by Chirag Rajdeo with Three.js and hand-written shaders. Sound, mobile tuning and the work pages come in later phases.
