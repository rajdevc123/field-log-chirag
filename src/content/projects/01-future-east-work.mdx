As a Creative Producer at **Future East Digital** (the commercials arm of award-winning film production house Future East Film), I spent 18 months orchestrating the end-to-end development and production of **over 20 commercial films** for top-tier multinational brands.

In this role, I juggled timelines, clients, and creative chaos to bring high-impact advertisements to life. Big ideas met tight turnarounds—coordinating directors, cinematographers, casting agencies, animators, VFX artists, and DI mastering suites to deliver polished commercial campaigns.
