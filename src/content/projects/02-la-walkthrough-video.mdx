This cinematic walkthrough film showcases Dr. Reddy’s **Leadership Academy**—a flagship architectural and learning environment dedicated to executive education, cross-functional collaboration, global leadership cohorts, town halls, and centralized enterprise inductions.

The video serves as an engaging introduction to the facility, engineered specifically for new hires and global teams across Dr. Reddy's **Europe and North America** operations who undergo virtual onboarding before visiting the physical campus.

## Rapid Video Production Sprint

The entire project was executed within a tight **10-day window**, covering all stages of production:

- **Scriptwriting & Creative Treatment**: Designing an energetic, conversational walkthrough structure that guides viewers through dynamic learning zones, amphitheaters, and collaborative pods.
- **Storyboarding & Shot Planning**: Formulating movement-based camera blocking optimized for steady gimbal movements and ambient architectural light.
- **Cinematography & Filming**: Captured over 2 production days utilizing cinema cameras, 3-axis electronic gimbals, prime lenses, and targeted spatial audio recording.
- **Post-Production & Sound Design**: Editing pacing, color grading in DaVinci Resolve, dynamic title animations in After Effects, and full audio mixing.
