Tangible Soundscapes reimagines digital sound synthesis through physical materiality—replacing screen-based sliders with weighted brass nodes, hall-effect sensors, and custom 3D-printed enclosures.

<Figure src="/projects/tangible-soundscapes/01.svg" caption="Custom PCB and 3D-printed enclosure housing ESP32 microcontroller and hall-effect sensor matrix." />
