I designed and developed a comprehensive three-course onboarding package for the **Horizon 2** division at Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories. The objective was to create a structured e-learning program that equips new employees with a clear understanding of Horizon 2’s culture, strategic vision, and execution pipeline.

This end-to-end solution ensures a seamless transition for team members into the company’s innovative products ecosystem. All courses were developed in **Articulate Storyline** as **SCORM-compliant** content, adhering strictly to **508 accessibility compliance** for an inclusive learning experience.

## 01 · Introduction to Innovative Workstreams & Products

This 30-minute module, led by Subject Matter Experts (SMEs), serves as an orientation to the Horizon 2 function. It outlines overarching commercial strategy, long-term vision, and real-world case studies across three primary verticals:

- **Consumer Products**
- **Digital Products**
- **Novel Assets**

The content was originally derived from extensive technical slide decks, which underwent multiple iterations of visual and information design to elevate clarity, cognitive pacing, and learner retention.

## 02 · Aspire Behaviors for Innovative Products

A focused video masterclass featuring **Mr. M.V. Ramana**, CEO of Branded Generics at Dr. Reddy’s, guiding the workforce through **ASPIRE**—the company’s culture code.

Internally recognized as the core values framework, this system was reimagined specifically for the high-stakes, fast-iteration environment of Horizon 2, translating corporate values into day-to-day creative problem-solving behaviors.

## 03 · Managing Innovation to Drive Innovative Products

This e-learning course provides employees with a deep, actionable understanding of the **Stage-Gate Process**—tracking the end-to-end lifecycle of innovative healthcare products from early ideation and feasibility through development, regulatory compliance, and commercial launch.

As the most extensive module in the curriculum, it equips employees with the operational frameworks required to navigate risk and collaboration.

The course content was synthesized from leadership strategy sessions conducted with international consulting partners. Working collaboratively with instructional designers, we structured the material into five comprehensive chapters:

1. **Innovation Ecosystem & Strategic Alignment**
2. **Stage-Gate Milestones & Risk Assessment**
3. **Cross-Functional Synergy & Commercial Gateways**
4. **Entrepreneurial Mindset & Creative Problem Solving**
5. **Interactive Scenario Evaluations & Knowledge Checks**
