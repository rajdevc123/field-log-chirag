A collection of comprehensive visual identity and communication systems created for Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories’ flagship organizational learning academies. These projects transformed internal learning programs into memorable, prestigious brand experiences with cohesive design systems across print, spatial, and digital touchpoints.

## 01 · New Horizons Leadership Program (NHLP)

The identity design for the **New Horizons Leadership Program (NHLP)** reimagines a decade-long leadership development initiative at Dr. Reddy’s.

With NHLP’s legacy of shaping future enterprise executives, the design objective was to refresh its visual language while preserving institutional continuity and emotional resonance with senior leaders.

## 02 · Guruskool — The Finance Academy

Brand identity and multimedia rollout for **Guruskool**, the centralized Finance & Accounting Academy at Dr. Reddy's.

The curriculum required a bold, approachable visual presence that demystified complex statutory accounting, compliance audits, transfer pricing, and fiscal governance for non-finance managers as well as seasoned accountants.
