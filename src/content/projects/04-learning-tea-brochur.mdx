I designed a **40-page publication booklet** for Dr. Reddy’s Learning & Development (L&D) leadership team, capturing their flagship development initiatives, organization-wide capability building programs, operational delivery models, and specialized studio services.

The publication served as a high-level strategic communication asset, distributed across business leaders and global functional heads to communicate the L&D organization’s roadmap and value creation.

## Rapid Editorial Production

This project spanned end-to-end editorial execution:

- **Concept & Editorial Direction**: Formulating an engaging editorial narrative that balances statistical proof points with employee learning journeys.
- **Content Aggregation**: Structuring multi-departmental program data into clean, digestible visual modules.
- **Layout & Typographic Systems**: Establishing rigorous modular baseline grids, hierarchy scales, and custom icon sets in Figma and Adobe InDesign.
- **Print Pre-Press & Quality Control**: Managing CMYK color separations, paper stock specifications, binding margins, and proofing for high-volume commercial press printing.
