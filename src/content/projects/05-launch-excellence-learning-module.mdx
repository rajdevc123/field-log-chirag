This e-learning course was developed for Dr. Reddy’s **Oncology Marketing Center of Excellence (COE)**, providing an interactive, structured overview of the end-to-end framework for launching innovative pharmaceutical products across several global geographies.

The course curriculum was translated from an extensive corporate playbook engineered by **McKinsey & Company** for Dr. Reddy’s. Through iterative instructional design cycles, we transformed dense management consulting documents into an intuitive, gamified digital experience.

<Figure
  src="/projects/launch-excellence-learning-module/thumb.png"
  caption="Course UI design system and global navigation layout built in Articulate Storyline."
/>

My role spanned visual identity, information architecture, cognitive sequencing, custom animations for complex pharmacology and market pathways, and gamified scenario mechanics.

Designed as a **45-minute branched learning simulation**, the course drove adoption of the commercial launch excellence pipeline across three critical functional units:

- **Commercial Strategy**
- **Market Access & Pricing**
- **Medical Affairs & Clinical Engagement**
