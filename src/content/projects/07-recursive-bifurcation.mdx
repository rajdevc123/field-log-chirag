Recursive Bifurcation investigates how simple deterministic rules fracture into complex organic structures when perturbed by human movement in physical space.

<Figure src="/projects/recursive-bifurcation/01.svg" caption="Real-time branching field responding to ultrasonic and optical tracking inputs." />

<TwoCol
  left="The installation pairs custom microcontroller sensor arrays with a real-time GLSL reaction-diffusion and branching pipeline. As visitors approach the projection plane, local bifurcation parameters shift—splitting singular trajectories into dense mycelial networks."
  right="Developed during the New Media Design studio at NID Gandhinagar, the system runs at 60fps with zero-latency serial communication between physical hardware and shader uniforms."
