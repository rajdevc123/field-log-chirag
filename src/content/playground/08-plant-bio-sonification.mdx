An exploration into non-human interactive modalities and plant electrophysiology. Two leaf-surface electrodes capture micro-voltage differentials across living plant tissue via a 16-bit ADS1115 ADC and Arduino. The telemetry stream is dynamically filtered, baseline-calibrated, and mapped in Processing to modulate a detuned drone choir, sweeping resonant formant filters, and generative spore particles in real time.
